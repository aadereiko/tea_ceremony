# Rooms and people

> **In short:** a room is one tasting. Anyone with its link and password can join. Your name is
> your identity in the room: join with the same name on another device and you continue as
> yourself.

## Make a room

1. On the home page press **+ New room**.
2. Fill in **Room name**, **Room password** (at least 4 characters) and **Your name**.
3. Pick a **Room icon** and **Your animal** if you like. They're just for fun.
4. Choose the room settings. See [Room settings](/docs/settings).

## Join a room

1. Open the invite link, or press **Join a room** on the home page and type the 8-letter room code
   (it looks like `k7m2pqxa`).
2. Type the room password and your name.

> **Tip:** the room code and the invite link are shown at the top of the room. Tap them to copy.

## Use the same name on your phone and laptop

There are no accounts in Tea Ceremony. Instead, **your name inside a room is you**.

- To continue on another device, join the room there with **exactly the same name**.
- Capitals and extra spaces don't matter: "Anna" and " anna " are the same person.
- You get back all your scores and notes.

> **Careful:** this also means anyone who knows the password could join with your name. Only share
> the password with people you trust.

## The host

The person who made the room is its **host**. Only the host can:

- add teas to the room,
- change the [room settings](/docs/settings),
- delete the room.

Everyone else can rate, reveal and hide.

## Leave or delete a room

- **Leave on this device** forgets the room on this phone or computer only. Your scores stay, and you
  can join again any time.
- **Delete room** (host only) removes the room, its teas and all ratings for everyone. It can't be
  undone. The teas stay in the [catalog](/docs/teas).

## Who's here

Tap the people icons at the top of the room to see who joined. There you can also **Change animal**
for yourself.
