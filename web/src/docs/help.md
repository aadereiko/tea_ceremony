# Privacy and help

> **In short:** there are no accounts and no emails. Each room is protected by its password, and
> your login is a random key kept in your browser.

## Privacy

- **Passwords** are stored only as a scrambled "hash" that can't be turned back into the password.
- **Your login** is a random key saved in this browser. The app keeps only a scrambled copy.
- **Too many wrong passwords:** after 10 wrong tries in 10 minutes, a room stops accepting joins for
  a while, so nobody can guess the password by trying many.
- **Map and address search** use OpenStreetMap. Shop addresses you search for are sent to it.

## Common questions

**I cleared my browser and my rooms are gone.**
Join the room again with the same name. You continue as yourself, with all your scores.

**Can I change the room password?**
Not yet.

**Someone revealed scores too early.**
Press **Hide scores** on that tea. Anyone in the room can do it.

**Why is a tea called "Mystery tea #3"?**
The room is a [blind tasting](/docs/settings#blind-tasting). Press **Reveal tea** to see its name.

**The ranking order looks odd. A tea with a 10 is below a 9.**
That's the [fair ranking](/docs/analytics#fair-ranking): one high score counts less than several
people agreeing.

## Version

The home page shows which version of the app is running. Mention it if you report a problem.
