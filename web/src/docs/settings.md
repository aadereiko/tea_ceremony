# Room settings

> **In short:** three switches decide what people can see while tasting. The host sets them when
> making the room and can change them any time from the room's **More** menu → **Room settings**.

## Blind tasting

**On by default.** Each tea shows as "Mystery tea #1", "#2" and so on, until someone presses
**Reveal tea**.

- Use it when you want to taste without being influenced by the name or the packaging.
- The tea's **notes** are still shown, because they usually hold the brewing steps (for example
  "6 g at 80 °C for 3 min"). So keep the tea's name out of the notes.
- The app doesn't even send the hidden name to your phone, so nobody can peek at it.

## Anonymous ratings

**Off by default.** When on:

- no names are shown next to scores or notes,
- the per-person parts of [Analytics](/docs/analytics) are hidden.

Use it when people might feel shy about giving a low score.

## Hide scores until revealed

When on, everyone's scores for a tea stay hidden until someone presses **Reveal scores** for that
tea. This stops people from copying each other.

- New rooms turn it **on** automatically if you choose anonymous ratings, and **off** otherwise.
  You can still change it.
- You always see your own score.

## Which settings should I use?

| You want… | Blind | Anonymous | Hide scores |
|---|---|---|---|
| A fun guessing game | On | Off | On |
| Honest scores from a shy group | Off | On | On |
| A relaxed evening, talking as you go | Off | Off | Off |
