# Getting started

> **In short:** one person makes a room, friends join it, you add the teas you're drinking, and
> everyone gives each tea a score. The app then shows which teas the group liked most.

## Your first tasting in 5 steps

1. **Make a room.** On the home page press **+ New room**. Give it a name (for example "Advent
   calendar 2026"), a password and your name. You are now the room's **host**.
2. **Invite friends.** In the room, press the link icon (**Copy invite link**) and send the link to
   your friends, together with the password.
3. **Add teas.** Press **Add tea**. Pick a tea from the catalog, or describe a new one.
4. **Rate.** Everyone opens a tea and taps scores from 0 to 10. Scores save by themselves.
5. **Reveal and compare.** When everyone has rated, press **Reveal scores** on the tea. Open
   **Analytics** to see the ranking.

## Words used in the app

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Room** | One tasting session, like an evening or a whole advent calendar. |
| **Host** | The person who made the room. Adds teas and changes the settings. |
| **Catalog** | The shared list of all teas and ingredients, used by every room. |
| **Mystery tea** | A tea whose name is hidden, so you taste it without knowing what it is. |
| **Reveal** | Show something that was hidden (a tea's name, or everyone's scores) to the whole room. |
| **Tea shop** | A place that sells tea. Anyone can add one, score it and comment on it. |
