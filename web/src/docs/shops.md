# Tea shops

> **In short:** a shared map and list of places to buy tea. Anyone can add a shop, give it a score
> and leave comments. Teas can be linked to the shops that sell them.

Open **Shops** in the bar at the top of any page.

## Add a shop

1. Press **+ Add shop**.
2. Fill in **Name**, **Country** and **City**. **Address** and **Website** are optional.
3. Put it on the map: press **Find address on map**, or tap the map where the shop is. Tap again to
   move the pin.
4. Press **Add shop**.

> **Good to know:** **Find address on map** asks OpenStreetMap's free search service, so the
> address you typed is sent to it.

## Score and comment

On a shop's page:

- **Your score:** tap 0 to 10. One score per person, and you can change it any time. Tap your score
  again to remove it.
- **Comments:** anything useful, like what they sell, prices, or whether it's worth the trip. You can
  delete your own comments.

The big number at the top is the shop's average score.

## Link teas to shops

Open a tea (in the catalog or a room), and under **Where to buy** pick the shops that sell it.

- The tea then shows **Buy at …** with links to those shops.
- The shop's page lists it under **Teas sold here**.
- A mystery tea keeps its shops hidden until it's revealed.

## Find shops

- Tap a pin on the map to open that shop.
- Search by name, city or address, or filter by country.
- **Directions** on a shop's page opens it on OpenStreetMap.

## Who am I here?

You score and comment as the person you are in your **first room on this device**. Your scores and
comments stay even if that room is deleted later.

A shop can only be deleted while nobody else has scored or commented on it.
