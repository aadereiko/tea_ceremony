# Rating and revealing

> **In short:** open a tea, tap your scores, done. Nothing to save. Others see your scores only
> once someone reveals them (if the room hides scores).

## Rate a tea

1. Tap a tea in the room.
2. Tap a number from **0 to 10** for each of these:
   - **Smell**: how much you like the aroma.
   - **Taste**: how much you enjoy the flavour while sipping.
   - **Finish**: how pleasant the taste is a few seconds after you swallow.
   - **Overall**: your total verdict. This is the score the ranking uses.
3. Optionally answer **Would buy again** and write a note.

Every change saves by itself. Tap a number again to clear it. You can change your rating any time.

> **Tip:** you don't have to fill in everything. An Overall score alone is fine.

## Reveal a tea

In a [blind tasting](/docs/settings#blind-tasting), press **Reveal tea** to show its name,
ingredients and colour to everyone. **Hide tea** hides it again.

## Reveal scores

If the room [hides scores](/docs/settings#hide-scores-until-revealed), press **Reveal scores** when
everyone has rated. Everyone's scores for that tea appear at once. **Hide scores** hides them again.

> **Good to know:** anyone in the room can reveal or hide, not only the host. Revealing affects
> everyone, so agree on it first.

## Your notes as a PDF

In [Analytics](/docs/analytics), under **Each taster**, press **PDF** next to a name to save that
person's notes as a PDF file.
